Our Process

Methodical,
by design.

The same careful sequence on every car, whether it's a weekend exotic or a work ute.

  1. 01

    Quote

    Tell us about your car and send a few photos. We reply with a price made for your vehicle, not a menu.

  2. 02

    Inspect

    A walk-around under proper light before anything starts. We note what's there and agree on what matters to you.

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Pre-rinse, decontaminate and protect the sensitive areas. The groundwork that prevents damage later.

  4. 04

    Detail

    The work itself, panel by panel and seam by seam, with the right tool for every surface.

  5. 05

    Final check

    A second inspection from every angle. Anything that isn't right gets redone before you see it.

  6. 06

    Handover

    We walk you through the finish and how to keep it that way.
